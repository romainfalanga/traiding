SPECIAL CASE: REGIME TRANSITION IN PROGRESS

The market is transitioning between regimes. During transitions:
- Use the MORE CONSERVATIVE of the two regime multipliers
- Require HIGHER confidence for new trades (add 0.1 to threshold)
- Monitor transition score: >0.7 means transition is nearly complete
- Avoid large positions until the new regime is confirmed
- Transition periods often have increased volatility — widen stops

Transition typically completes within 4-24 hours.
